After you sign in

These are the main pages, grouped as the sidebar groups them. A member of an organisation sees them under the organisation's address: for the organisation at /o/acme, the catalog is at /o/acme/catalog. A page marked "if you are allowed" opens only for the people the portal's administrators have chosen.
