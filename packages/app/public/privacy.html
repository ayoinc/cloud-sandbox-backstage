Cloud Sandbox

Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Cloud Sandbox is a developer portal. It lists software, APIs and documentation for the teams that use it. This notice explains what the portal learns about you when you sign in, and what it does with it.

Who runs the portal

The Cloud Sandbox platform team runs this portal at backstage.intellidevops.me. To ask a question or use any of your rights below, email ayoinc@gmail.com.

What we collect

You sign in with a GitHub or Google account. We ask that provider who you are and receive only the details below. We never see your password, and we do not ask for access to your repositories, files, contacts or email.

If you sign in with We receive
GitHub Your username, display name, profile picture address and public email address, if you have one
Google Your email address, name and profile picture address

While you use the portal we also keep:

  • the preferences you set, such as your theme and the items you star
  • technical request logs: the page or API address requested, the time, the response status and your network address

How we use it

  • To check that you are allowed to use the portal.
  • To show your name and picture in the portal, to you and to the other people signed in to it: for example as the person who created a component or started a template.
  • To remember your preferences between visits.
  • To keep the portal secure and to find and fix faults.

We do not sell your details, use them for advertising, build profiles of you or use them to train AI models.

Cookies

The portal sets only the cookies it needs to work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. It holds your username or email address, name and picture address, and is protected so that it cannot be read by scripts or altered.
  • A short-lived cookie, kept for up to 10 minutes, protects the sign-in step itself.

Where it is kept and who else handles it

The portal runs on Cloudflare, which stores your preferences and the request logs on our behalf. GitHub and Google handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies. We share your details with nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your sign-in ends when you sign out, or after 7 days.
  • Your name, picture address and email address are kept as your entry in the portal's list of users until you ask us to delete it.
  • Your preferences are kept until you remove them or ask us to delete them.
  • Request logs are kept for a short period set by Cloudflare and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. You can stop the portal receiving your details at any time by removing its access in your GitHub settings or your Google Account. If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this notice

If we change how the portal uses your details we will update this page and the date at the top.
