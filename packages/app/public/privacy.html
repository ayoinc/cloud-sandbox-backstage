Developer portal

Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is a developer portal. It lists software, APIs and documentation for the teams that use it. This notice explains what the portal learns about you when you sign in, and what it does with it.

The portal is run by the organisation or team that gave you access to it. "We" in this notice means them. To ask a question or use any of your rights below, contact the person who sent you this portal's address.

What we collect

You sign in with a GitHub or Google account, whichever this portal offers. To sign you in we ask that provider only who you are, and keep only the details below. We never see your password. On some portals a page that shows a component's builds asks GitHub for access to your repositories the first time you open it; GitHub shows you the request and you can refuse it. If the portal offers a guest account and you choose it, we receive nothing from GitHub or Google: everyone who uses the guest account shares it.

If you sign in with We keep
GitHub Your username, display name, profile picture address and public email address, if you have one
Google Your email address, name and profile picture address

While you use the portal we also keep:

  • the preferences you save in the portal, such as the items you star. Your theme and a few other settings stay in your browser only; the cookies page lists them
  • the pages you open in the portal: the name and address of up to 100, when you last opened each and how often. The Home page uses them to show you the pages you visited recently and most
  • technical request logs: the page or API address requested, the time, the response status and your network address, and your user name when you sign out or a sign-in is refused

If you create or join an organisation in the portal, we keep your membership: your user name, your role, who added you and when. If someone invites you to an organisation, we keep the invitation: your user name, the role offered, who invited you and when. The members of that organisation can see both. A person you invite sees your user name, and the people who run the portal can see who owns each organisation. An organisation's name, logo and theme are public: its sign-in page shows them before anyone has signed in.

How we use it

  • To check that you are allowed to use the portal.
  • To show your name, picture and email address in the portal: to you, and to the members of your organisation if you are in one, in the list of users, in search, and as the person who created a component or started a template. On some portals the people who run it list everyone who may sign in, and that list may be shown to everyone signed in.
  • To remember your preferences between visits.
  • To keep the portal secure and to find and fix faults.

We do not sell your details, use them for advertising, build profiles of you or use them to train AI models.

Cookies

The portal sets only the cookies it needs to work: there are no advertising or analytics cookies. The cookies page lists each one, the other settings kept in your browser, and how to remove them.

Counting visits

This portal counts visits. The counting sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser and records nothing that identifies you. The cookies page lists what it records.

This portal does not count visits.

Where it is kept and who else handles it

Your details, your preferences and your organisation are stored in the portal's database, and the request logs on the servers the portal runs on, both chosen by whoever runs the portal. The account provider you sign in with, GitHub or Google, handles your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

When you run a template, the portal may hand the run to an automation service, such as GitHub Actions, in a repository that belongs to whoever runs the portal. The run fetches what you entered in the form from the portal and uses it to do the work; the run's own record names the template and the steps, not you, and holds the error if the run fails. If whoever runs the portal has turned on full logs, people who can see that repository's runs can also read what the template's steps print. We share your details with nobody else, unless the law requires it.

The service that counts visits holds those counts on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Your sign-in ends when you sign out, when the portal has been open but unused for one hour, or when it expires.
  • Your name, picture address and email address are kept as your entry in the portal's list of users. It can be written again when you next sign in, so to remove it for good, ask us to delete it and stop using the portal.
  • The list of pages you opened is kept until you ask us to delete it; older entries drop off after 100.
  • Your preferences are kept until you change them back in the portal or ask us to delete them.
  • Request logs are kept for as long as the servers the portal runs on are set to keep them.
  • Your membership of an organisation is kept until you leave, an owner removes you, or the organisation is deleted. An invitation is kept until you answer it, an owner takes it back, or the organisation is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. You can stop the portal receiving your details at any time by removing its access in your GitHub settings or your Google Account. If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this notice

If we change how the portal uses your details we will update this page and the date at the top.
