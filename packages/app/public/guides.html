Guides

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Short instructions for the things people do most often in the portal. The links to pages inside the portal need you to be signed in. If you belong to an organisation, the portal moves you to the same page under your organisation's address.

Sign in

  1. Open the sign-in page. If your organisation has an address of its own, such as /o/acme, you can sign in there instead: it is the same portal.
  2. Choose GitHub or Google, whichever the page offers. Some portals also offer a guest account, which is one account shared by everyone who uses it.
  3. A window opens at GitHub or Google. Sign in there. The portal asks only who you are.
  4. The window closes and the portal opens at the catalog, or at your organisation's address if you belong to one. The first time, if you have no organisation, it opens Settings at the Organisation tab.

The portal signs you out after one hour without activity. To sign out yourself, open Settings at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Sign Out. The privacy notice says what the portal receives when you sign in, and the cookies page says what it keeps in your browser.

Create an organisation or join one

An organisation gives the portal a name, logos and a theme at an address of its own. You can belong to one organisation at a time. The guest account cannot create or join one.

Create one

  1. Open Settings, then Organisation.
  2. Enter a name of up to 80 characters, and an address: 3 to 40 lower-case letters, digits and dashes, starting and ending with a letter or digit. It becomes /o/your-address and cannot be changed later.
  3. Create the organisation. You are its first owner, and the portal moves you to its address.
  4. On the same tab, add the logos and choose the theme, then invite people by their user name in the portal. Each of them chooses whether to join.

Join one

  1. Ask an owner of the organisation to invite you.
  2. Open Settings, then Organisation. The invitation is shown there.
  3. Join, or decline. If you already belong to another organisation, leave it first: until then you can only decline.

Find a component, API or document

  1. To browse, open the catalog. The sidebar has a shortcut for each kind of entry, such as Components and APIs. Open an entry to see its owner, its relations and its documentation.
  2. To look for a word, choose Search at the top of the sidebar, or open the search page. Search covers catalog entries and documentation.
  3. To read documentation, open Docs and choose a component, or open the Docs tab on a component's own page.
  4. To read an API's definition, open the API explorer and choose the API.

Create something from a template

  1. Choose Create... in the sidebar, or open the list of templates.
  2. Choose a template.
  3. Fill in the form. Each template asks for what it needs, such as a name, an owner and where the new repository goes.
  4. Check the summary and create.
  5. The portal shows each step of the run as it happens. When the run has finished, follow its links to what it made. Your earlier runs are listed under Tasks.

If Create... is missing from your sidebar, your account is not allowed to run templates.

Register a component that already exists

Registering adds a repository you already have to the catalog. Not everyone may do it: the people who run the portal decide who. If Register Existing Component is missing from your sidebar, or the page refuses you, ask them.

  1. Add a catalog-info.yaml file that describes the component to its repository on GitHub.
  2. Open Register an existing component.
  3. Paste the GitHub link to the file and follow the steps.
  4. Find the component in the catalog. It can take a few minutes to appear.

Get help

To ask a question, report a fault or ask for access, contact the person who sent you this portal's address. The sitemap lists the portal's main pages.
