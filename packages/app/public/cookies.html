Cookies

Last updated: 11 November 2026

A cookie is a small file that a website saves in your browser. This page lists what the portal keeps in your browser: cookies, and a few settings that are not cookies. Each is either needed for the portal to work or remembers a setting, so the portal does not ask for your consent before it sets them. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies.

Cookies the portal sets

The portal sets cookies for three purposes. Their exact names and how long they last depend on how the portal you are using has been set up; your browser's settings list the ones it holds for this address.

Cookie What it is for How long it lasts
Sign-in protection, such as github-nonce or google-nonce Protects the sign-in step. It holds a random value that the portal checks when GitHub or Google sends you back, so that a sign-in can be finished only in the browser that started it. A few minutes
Sign-in Keeps you signed in, so that you are not asked again on every page. Until you sign out, or until it expires
Documentation, backstage-auth Lets documentation pages load their images and files while you are signed in. About an hour. It is renewed while you read documentation.

Scripts in the page cannot read these cookies.

Things kept in your browser that are not cookies

The portal also keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage. They stay in your browser and are not sent to the portal with your requests.

Name What it is for
@backstage/core:SignInPage:provider The sign-in method you chose, so that the portal can sign you in the same way on your next visit.
organisation-shown Your user name, and which prompts about organisations this browser has already shown you: the form that creates one, and each invitation. The portal uses it to show each of them once.
sidebarPinState Whether you have pinned the sidebar open.
theme The theme you chose in Settings, if you chose one.
featureFlags The feature flags you switched on in Settings, if any.
language The language the portal is shown in. It is set on your first visit.

Counting visits

This portal counts visits. The counting sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser.

For each page you open it records:

  • the page address, including anything after a ?, such as the words you searched for
  • the page that linked to it
  • the kind of browser and device
  • the country, worked out from your network address
  • how fast the page loaded

It does not record who you are and does not follow you to other sites.

This portal does not count visits.

How to remove cookies and settings

  • Sign out, from Settings in the sidebar. This removes the cookie that keeps you signed in. The documentation cookie stays until it expires, within the hour.
  • Clear the site data for this address in your browser's settings. This removes every cookie and setting listed on this page, and signs you out.

If you block cookies

Sign-in does not work without these cookies, so you cannot use the portal if your browser blocks them for this address. This page, the privacy notice, the guides and the sitemap can still be read.
